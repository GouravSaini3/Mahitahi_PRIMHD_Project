Tier 1 — System Administrators

Data Team · Highest access

Full ownership of Recordbase: configuration, governance and operational control, including the permissions no other tier holds.

What you can do

  • View, create, edit, delete and administer every open people-management module
  • Delete referrals and delete people
  • Roll back referrals to previous states
  • Manage user accounts and system roles
  • Create, view and download data extracts
  • Configure settings, goal templates, activity templates, referrers, referral reasons and service units
  • Administer teams, group activities and allocated hours
  • Create inbound and outbound referrals; Employers full access

What you cannot do

  • Direct messages, injury claims, prescriptions and protocols are closed
  • Notes agent — create notes on behalf of others
  • MyRecord Administrator
  • Foster care features
  • eReferrals administration
  • Incident administrator control
  • Protocol templates and private people administration
  • Bulk deletion of scheduled activities

What changes as a referral moves on

Referral stateYour access
Open referralsFull access — view, new, edit, delete, admin
Recently dischargedFull access
Closed referralsFull access

Why this tier needs the tightest control

  • Every high-risk permission in Recordbase sits in this tier. Membership should be the smallest number of named people that keeps the service running.
  • All Tier 1 activity must be covered by audit logging.
  • Tier 1 holders are reviewed quarterly, and access is removed immediately on role change or exit.
  • Deletion and rollback are irreversible in practice — confirm the process for authorising them.
