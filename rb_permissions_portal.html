📋 Project Overview

Current Status

Phase: Preparation & Distribution (15-17 September 2026)

Next Milestone: Feedback collection opens 17 September

We're implementing seven clearly defined permission tiers in Recordbase to replace legacy roles and improve security, privacy, and audit compliance. This portal brings together all the information you need to understand the changes, review your proposed access, and provide feedback.

What's changing?

  • Legacy Recordbase roles replaced with seven clear, role-aligned tiers
  • Each user assigned one tier with documented permissions
  • High-risk functions (deletion, templates, settings) centralized with Tier 1
  • Tier 7 introduced: Referral Creator Only (for staff who create referrals only)
  • One-week feedback period before live implementation
  • Hypercare (Active monitoring & support) support available for 10 days after go-live

The seven tiers

🔐 Tier 1

System Administrators

Full access. Manage permissions, templates, settings, and deletions.

👤 Tier 2

User Account Administrators

Manage user accounts, referral entry, extracts, referrer management.

👥 Tier 3

Service Delivery (Managers & Team Leaders)

Manage team members and cases. Limited editing and reporting access.

👨 Tier 4

CSW Standard Users (Kaimahi)

Create and manage referrals and cases. View-only on transfers (team leads process).

📊 Tier 5

Service Delivery (Directors)

View-only access for governance and decision-making visibility.

🔍 Tier 6

Auditors/Funders

Restricted audit and reporting access to meet compliance requirements.

✍️ Tier 7

Referral Creator Only

Limited access: Create referrals for their assigned team only. This tier is intended solely for staff who need to create referrals as part of their role. Access will only be granted following approval from the Quality Team and does not include any broader manager or administrative permissions.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why are we changing permission tiers?

Legacy Recordbase roles have accumulated over time and no longer provide clear, consistent access levels aligned to user responsibilities. The new seven-tier system improves record quality, protects privacy, meets audit/funder requirements, and ensures access is least-privileged and role-based.

Will I lose access to things I need?

That's exactly why we're asking for your feedback during the one-week review period. If you believe a permission required for your role has been removed, we want to hear about it with your justification. The Data and Quality teams will review all feedback and make adjustments before go-live.

What's Tier 7 – Referral Creator Only?

Tier 7 has been created for staff who are responsible for creating referrals for their team. Access is limited to referral creation functions only, helping maintain security, privacy, and least-privilege principles. This access may also be granted temporarily to approved staff, following the access request process that will be provided separately by the Quality Team.

What happens if something goes wrong on 1 October?

Hypercare support (Active monitoring & Support) will be available from 1-9 October. Access issues are monitored and triaged within 2 hours; fixes are applied the same working day where they align with the confirmed permission model. A rollback plan is ready if a High-severity issue affects a whole tier and cannot be fixed same day.

Who do I contact with questions?

Before 1 October (feedback & planning): Contact the Project Lead (Gourav Saini), Service Coordinator (Meegan Herewini)
1-9 October (Active monitoring & Support): Reach out to Service Coordinator (Meegan Herewini), Data Team or contact your team leader or manager
After 9 October: Contact Quality Team for access review requests

Can I request a temporary access to another tier after go-live?

Yes. The Quality Team manages a formal process for requesting temporary access to a different permission tier. Process details will be shared by Quality Team.
